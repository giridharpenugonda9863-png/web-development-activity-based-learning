Smart Travel Tips

Plan better and travel responsibly.

  1. Keep your identification and travel documents safe.
  2. Check the weather and local conditions before travelling.
  3. Respect local customs, traditions and religious places.
  4. Carry drinking water, necessary medicines and comfortable shoes.
  5. Keep emergency contacts and hotel details available.
  6. Protect nature and avoid littering at tourist destinations.
